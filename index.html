CTEM Workflow

Continuous Exposure Management

The documented workflow continuously moves security exposure from discovery toward validated remediation.

  • 1. Scoping — define the assets, applications and environments in scope.
  • 2. Discovery — discover vulnerabilities, secrets, misconfigurations and other exposures.
  • 3. Prioritization — rank exposures using risk and business context.
  • 4. Validation — validate whether identified exposures are exploitable or require action.
  • 5. Mobilization — route remediation and track the security improvement cycle.
